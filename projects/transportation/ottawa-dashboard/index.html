Understanding crossing protection types

Active – FLBG
Flashing lights, bells, and automatic gates. The highest level of active warning — gates physically block the road when a train approaches.
Active – FLB
Flashing lights and bells, but no gates. Drivers are warned automatically, but nothing physically prevents crossing the tracks.
Passive
Static signage only (e.g. a stop or yield sign) — no automatic warning of an approaching train. Relies entirely on the driver looking and listening.
